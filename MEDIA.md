### Design direction

The visual uses the project’s dark cyber-intelligence language with high-contrast typography, technical panels, graph and telemetry motifs, and the **Protect. Detect. Defend.** identity. It is intended as a brand overview rather than a substitute for source documentation, formal certification records, or independent validation of professional claims.
