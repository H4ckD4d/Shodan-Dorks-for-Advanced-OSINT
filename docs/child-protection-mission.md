# Child Protection Mission

> **Project owner / original creator / primary maintainer: Chris Cruz | h4ckd4d**

## Mission

Project h4ckd4d applies cybersecurity, OSINT, threat intelligence, digital investigation methods, and public-awareness work to help make the digital environment safer for children and families.

This mission is inspired by the public-facing child-protection work presented through **rifleman.us** and is intentionally separated from the technical EASM/OSINT framework so that the repository remains useful to security analysts while preserving the broader social purpose behind the project.

## Focus areas

### Online grooming awareness

Help parents, guardians, educators, and responsible adults recognize common patterns of manipulation, impersonation, coercion, and unsafe contact involving minors in online environments.

### Digital threats affecting minors

Promote awareness of risks such as sextortion, cyberbullying, doxxing, impersonation, account compromise, privacy exposure, and social-engineering pressure.

### OSINT for responsible support

Use open-source intelligence methods ethically to organize public information, preserve context, validate digital evidence, and support responsible escalation to appropriate authorities or qualified organizations.

### Missing-person and family-support context

Promote careful preservation of digital evidence, timeline reconstruction, account/context review, and responsible information sharing without replacing formal law-enforcement or emergency response.

### Threat intelligence and prevention

Translate cyber threat intelligence into practical prevention guidance for families, organizations, schools, and security teams.

## Operating principles

1. **Protect first.** Safety, privacy, and harm reduction come before curiosity or technical experimentation.
2. **Preserve evidence.** Do not alter or contaminate material that may be relevant to an investigation.
3. **Minimize exposure.** Collect and retain only information necessary for a legitimate defensive purpose.
4. **Verify before concluding.** Public data is evidence to assess, not automatic proof of identity, ownership, intent, or wrongdoing.
5. **Escalate responsibly.** Emergencies, suspected crimes, and immediate threats belong with the appropriate authorities and qualified professionals.
6. **No impersonation of authority.** Project h4ckd4d and rifleman.us are independent initiatives and do not represent or speak for government, police, intelligence, or law-enforcement agencies.

## Relationship to this repository

The technical framework in this repository contributes reusable defensive capabilities that can also support child-protection-oriented analysis when properly authorized:

- Internet-facing asset attribution;
- public-source intelligence correlation;
- infrastructure relationship analysis;
- timeline and evidence normalization;
- confidence scoring;
- defensive reporting;
- threat-context enrichment.

The same analytical rule applies:

> **Observation ≠ Finding ≠ Vulnerability ≠ Exploitability ≠ Compromise**

For child-protection work, an additional principle is equally important:

> **A digital signal is not proof of a person's identity, intent, or guilt.**
